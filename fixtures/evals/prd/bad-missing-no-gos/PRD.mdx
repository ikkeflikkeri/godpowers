# PRD: Invoice Reminder Service

## Problem

- [FACT] Freelancers on the platform send 4,200 invoices per month and 31
  percent are paid late.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|----|-------------|----------|
| P-MUST-01 | Send a reminder email 3 days before an invoice due date | MUST |

## Success Metrics

- [DECISION] Late-payment rate drops from 31 percent to 22 percent within 90
  days of launch, measured by the weekly billing warehouse job that compares
  paid-at against due-at per invoice.

## Open Questions

- [HYPOTHESIS] Senders want a per-client quiet-hours window. Owner: PM
  (Dana). Due: 2026-08-20.
