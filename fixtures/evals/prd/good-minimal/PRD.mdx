# PRD: Invoice Reminder Service

## Problem

- [FACT] Freelancers on the platform send 4,200 invoices per month and 31
  percent are paid late.
- [FACT] Support logged 87 tickets in June 2026 asking for automatic payment
  reminders.

## Users

- [FACT] Primary: solo freelancers who invoice 5 to 20 clients per month.
- [DECISION] Agencies with more than 3 seats are out of scope for V1.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|----|-------------|----------|
| P-MUST-01 | Send a reminder email 3 days before an invoice due date | MUST |
| P-MUST-02 | Send a reminder email 2 days after a missed due date | MUST |
| P-SHOULD-01 | Let the sender pick reminder cadence per client | SHOULD |

## Success Metrics

- [DECISION] Late-payment rate drops from 31 percent to 22 percent within 90
  days of launch, measured by the weekly billing warehouse job that compares
  paid-at against due-at per invoice.
- [DECISION] Reminder opt-out stays under 5 percent within 60 days of launch,
  measured by the unsubscribe events table in the analytics pipeline.

## No-Gos

- [DECISION] No SMS reminders in V1: email covers 96 percent of recorded
  client contacts and SMS adds a compliance surface we cannot staff.
- [DECISION] No automatic late fees: charging money without explicit sender
  action is a trust risk we will not take in V1.
